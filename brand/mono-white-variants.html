The palette

Three blues and black. Black is a stop here, not a background.

Sky#5A8FE8 · the edges
Azure#2E5697 · middle step
Deep#16305C · before black
Black#000 · the base layer

Sky is the same value as the product's accent. #5A8FE8 is already the waveform bars, the spinner and the done-check. Picking a second, nearly-identical blue for the logo is how a palette starts drifting — nobody can tell them apart, so nobody keeps them in sync.

Black under the housing, charcoal at the wings

The middle overlaps the physical camera housing, so anything but true black there is a visible seam. The ramp finishes by 32%, so the colour is already black before the hardware starts rather than at its edge. Its wings stay charcoal, not #5A8FE8 — that lift is fine on a logo, but in a menu bar it turns hardware into a widget.
