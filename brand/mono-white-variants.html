Black under the housing, charcoal at the wings

The ramp finishes by 32%, so the colour is already black before the hardware starts rather than at its edge. The eye finds a seam far more easily than a ramp — and the middle of this shape overlaps the physical camera housing, so anything but true black there gives the trick away.

The fillets moved too. They fill the corner against a wing’s outer edge, so leaving them black would have drawn a dark notch either side of the shape — the exact seam they exist to remove.

Footer mark hanging from the border reverted

Doctrinally right, visually dead: the section above the footer is also dark, so a 64px mark landed on a dark-on-dark seam and vanished. It went back in the column — being visible beats being correct.

A wide top-edge highlight reverted ×3

A highlight as wide as the plate reaches both edges at the same height, and the eye reads any edge-to-edge change as a ramp however soft it is. The fix is a highlight taller than it is wide, running off the top and bottom.
